# BIG IDLE HEROES

**An idle party RPG that lives in a small battle window on your screen.**
Build a party of five heroes, send them along a route through the original game's zones, dungeons and raids, and let them fight on their own while you quest, raid, or sit in a queue. Drop in now and then to equip loot, spend talent and skill points, and push into the next stage.

> **Language:** the in-game interface is **Korean only** for now. The add-on runs on any client language and uses the Korean font that every WoW client includes, so the text always displays, but there is no English translation yet.

## Features

### Your party
- Up to **5 heroes in a fight: 1 tank, 3 damage dealers, 1 healer** (up to 12 heroes on your roster).
- Pick **Alliance or Horde** at the start. Each class takes its classic race for your faction. Heroes share the gender of the character you are logged in with.
- **All 9 original classes and 28 specializations**: Arms, Fury and Protection Warrior; Holy, Protection and Retribution Paladin; Guardian, Feral, Balance and Restoration Druid; and so on.
- Every hero gets a random "first name + family name" that fits its race and gender.
- **Talents** (stat points, one per level) and **skills** are separate. Skills unlock in the order the original game teaches them, and you equip up to three of them (more with runes). Every spec opens with a skill that fights (or heals).
- **Twelve stats**, easy to read: Attack, Health, Armor and Attack & cast speed; Critical chance, Critical damage, Boss damage and Cooldown reduction; Damage taken reduction, Elemental resistance, Dodge and Healing received. Healers heal out of their Attack.

### The world: 120 stages
- A route through **the original game's zones, dungeons and raids in recommended-level order**: Elwynn Forest, Dun Morogh, the Deadmines, Scarlet Monastery, Uldaman, Blackrock Depths, Scholomance, Stratholme, Zul'Gurub, Ahn'Qiraj, and on to Molten Core, Onyxia's Lair, Blackwing Lair, the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj and **Naxxramas**.
- **Four difficulty tiers** (Normal, Heroic, Mythic, Mythic+), each with 30 stages in three acts.
- Every stage is a long run of monster waves ending in a boss, with stage levels from 1 to 95. The last stage of each act is its raid boss alone, with its own abilities.
- A long climb, designed for about 250 hours from the first stage to the end of Mythic+: about 32 hours for Normal, 43 for Heroic, 55 for Mythic and 120 for Mythic+. In the endless tower every hero reaches level 100 in about ten hours.

### A living battle window
- Heroes, monsters, bosses, summons and companions are **the game's own 3D models**, animated: they stand, walk, attack, cast, get hit and die.
- Heroes' gear grows with your progress: they start in their class's **starting clothes**, then wear the **best gear of the level 20s** (Defias Leather and Embrace of the Viper on leather wearers), the **dungeon sets**, the **Ahn'Qiraj raid sets** and finally the **Naxxramas sets**, with matching weapons for each spec. Every difficulty you clear to its end moves every hero to the next look.
- Hunters shoot: a real bow or crossbow shot with an arrow flying to the target. Casters and healers cast at their target instead of swinging.
- Druids fight in **bear, cat or moonkin form**. You can turn forms off.
- Skills and boss abilities play **the original spells' visual effects**: casts, missiles and impacts.
- **Moving scenery:**
  - Each place has its own backdrop picture that scrolls by while the party walks between waves.
  - The ground is the zone's real terrain texture (Elwynn grass, Durotar red dirt, Dun Morogh snow, or a dungeon's own floor) and rolls by under the heroes' feet.
  - Outdoors, the zone's own trees, bushes and rocks pass in three parallax rows. Inside dungeons you see the dungeon's own props instead: Scholomance bookshelves and cauldrons, Blackrock Depths statues and anvils, Blackwing Lair dragon eggs.
- Melee summons walk beside their summoner and charge in with the melee heroes when a fight starts. Ranged summons stay beside their summoner. Your companion follows at the back.
- **Drag** the window anywhere, **resize** it from its corner, or switch to a compact view.

### Loot and progression
- **Gear in 10 grades**, from Common to Arcana (the top grade's names and item frames run through cyan, periwinkle and orchid), in fixed item levels (1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40, 50, 65, 80, 100). A hero wears an item from its own level, so gear above your heroes waits in the bag until they catch up.
- **Three kinds of chests**: monster chests, boss chests and act boss chests. Each stage has its own chests, drop chances and contents, shown on the stage screen.
- **The Forge**, a screen of its own: every function in a list on the left (salvage, fuse, craft, gems, enchant, inscription, lucky seals, materials, enhance), the work in the middle, and on the right the hero and item you work on plus a log of the latest results. Bags fill up, so auto-salvage helps keep them clear.
- **Gems, enchanting and inscription** set stats in gear sockets, each material in a line of ten grades: 5 gem lines, 4 enchanting lines and 4 scroll lines, each giving one stat on weapons, one on armor and one on accessories. A line gives the same three stats at every grade, only stronger; nine of a line fuse into one of the same line a grade up. One socket of any stat is worth about the same.
- Random stats on gear take one of five evenly spaced whole values.
- **Enhancement** (+1 to +20) once Mythic+ opens: every step raises an item's base stats; any try can fail (only the gold is lost): 65% a step up to +10, then lower and lower, down to 10% for the last step. Inheritance moves an enhanced item's steps onto a new item of the same slot, as 70% of the gold they are worth, with its gems, enchants and inscriptions; the old item is used up. The tooltip shows the step before the name (+10 Iron Helm); item icons show the item level.
- **Rune tree**: 197 account-wide runes bought with gold, spreading from the center of a map you zoom with the mouse wheel and drag around, one branch per kind: gold, experience, combat and chest runes leave the center on the diagonals, bag runes go straight up, forge runes to the left and unlock runes to the right. They open party seats, skill slots, offline rewards, automatic chest opening, bigger bags and more.
- **Companions** that join the party.
- **Offline rewards** for the time you were away: what your party earns hunting the highest stage it has cleared.
- A **"Recommended" hint** always points at the next useful thing to do, and red dots show where something is waiting.

### Stage screen
- World and stage pictures are **the game's own loading screens**: Forever's continent screens for outdoor zones, and the original dungeon and raid screens.

## Getting started

1. Install the add-on and enable **BIG IDLE HEROES** in the AddOns list at character select.
2. Log in. Choose **Alliance or Horde**, then create your first hero (class, specialization, name). Your first hero is free.
3. The fight starts on its own in the battle window. Open the menu at any time with **/bih**.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/bih` | Open or close the menu |
| `/bih bar` | Show or hide the battle window |
| `/bih reset` | Put the battle window back in its default place and size |

Right-click the battle window to open the menu. Chat messages from the add-on start with **[B.I.H]**.

## Settings

Open the menu, then **Settings**, to:

- change window sizes and lock the battle window;
- turn 3D models, druid forms and the moving scenery on or off;
- set auto-salvage, sound and your faction.

## Updating from an older version (BIG W.O.W IDLE)

The add-on was renamed, and its folder is now `BigIdleHeroes`. To keep your progress:

1. Exit the game completely.
2. Delete the old `Interface/AddOns/BigWowIdle` folder.
3. In `WTF/Account/<your account>/SavedVariables/`, copy `BigWowIdle.lua` to a new file named `BigIdleHeroes.lua`.
4. Start the game. Your heroes, gear and progress load as before.

**After installing or updating, always restart the game fully.** `/reload` does not pick up new image files.

## Notes

- Made for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. If the AddOns list marks it as out of date, tick "Load out of date AddOns".
- The battle runs while you play. It costs little, and you can hide the battle window at any time with `/bih bar`.

## Changelog for this release (v1.2.0)

```
Twelve stats
- Every hero now has twelve stats in three groups:
  Basic: Attack, Health, Armor, Attack & cast speed
  Offense: Critical chance, Critical damage, Boss damage, Cooldown reduction
  Defense: Damage taken reduction, Elemental resistance, Dodge,
  Healing received
- Gone: movement speed, block, absorb, lifesteal, regeneration, health on
  hit and on kill, thorns, multistrike, school damage, healing done,
  +all skill levels and the experience and gold gear stats. Talents, skill
  buffs, spec stats and weapon types give one of the twelve instead.
- Attack & cast speed runs skill cooldowns down as much faster as attacks.
- Boss damage: more damage to stage, act and tower bosses.
- One Elemental resistance for every element (up to 75%).
- Healing received: heals and shields on the hero grow by it.
- Heroes all walk at one pace.

Healers
- Healers heal (and shield) out of their Attack, and their heals can be
  critical. Skill texts show the heal as a share of the hero's Attack.
- The five healing specs were balanced against each other again.

Gear
- Random stats: weapons roll offense, armor rolls defense, accessories a
  mix. Every random stat takes one of five evenly spaced whole values,
  shown with its range.
- Gear score: Cooldown reduction counts as much as your equipped skills
  have cooldowns, and updates when you swap skills. Salvage protection also
  keeps gear that would be better with every skill slot on a cooldown
  skill.

Gems, enchanting reagents and scrolls
- 5 gem lines (ruby, sapphire, emerald, topaz, amethyst), 4 enchanting
  lines and 4 scroll lines; every stat comes from three or four lines.
- Values set grade by grade so one socket of any stat raises a hero about
  as much as any other. Scrolls roll one of five values too.

Skills
- Every spec opens with a skill that fights or heals: Arms Charge,
  Protection Paladin Seal of Righteousness, Assassination Garrote, Holy
  Priest Lesser Heal, Feral Claw. Arcane gets Mana Shield (Arcane Missiles
  first) and Guardian gets Bash in place of Thorns.

Combat runes
- The twenty combat runes now give the twelve stats, all as percentages
  for every hero: Attack, Health, Armor, Attack & cast speed, Critical
  chance, Critical damage, Boss damage, Cooldown reduction, Damage taken
  reduction, Elemental resistance, Dodge and Healing received.
- Every level is worth about the same for its gold price whatever stat it
  gives, and the whole branch is about as strong as before. Cells, links,
  levels and prices stay the same; the wave reduction rune is a stat rune
  now. Runes you learned keep their levels.

Experience and gold runes
- The runes that gave a flat amount of experience or gold per kill, per
  boss or per act boss now give +5% experience or +5% monster gold per
  level, so they keep their worth at every stage.
- Monsters give a little less experience and gold to make up for it:
  learning the runes as you go, heroes level and gold comes in about as
  fast as before.

Balance
- Monsters give more experience from the third act of Normal on, so heroes
  reach the levels they did before the stat overhaul (most of all in Mythic
  and Mythic+). The first two acts of Normal are unchanged.

Screens
- Hero screen, Stats: Basic, Offense and Defense, plus estimated damage per
  second and how much physical damage your armor stops at the current
  stage. The Cooldown reduction tooltip counts your cooldown skills.
- Equipment screen: all twelve stats in the hero summary; scroll them with
  the mouse wheel.

Saves
- Your gear, gems and materials move to the new stats: an old random stat
  becomes the nearest new one where it sat in its range; gems take their
  line's new stat; points in a replaced first skill go to the new skill.
```
